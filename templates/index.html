Emergency Medical Assistance

If you or someone near you is experiencing severe symptoms, please call emergency services immediately:

Call 911 US / Canada Call 112 Europe / India Call 988 Mental Health Lifeline

Critical Warning Signs (Seek ER immediately):

  • Chest pressure, squeezing, or pain radiating to jaw or left arm
  • Sudden difficulty breathing or choking
  • Sudden numbness, weakness on one side of face/body
  • Sudden confusion or inability to speak
  • Uncontrollable bleeding or severe head trauma
